How to set up

  1. Fix the phone straight above the board on a stand. It must not move during the game.
  2. Light the board evenly. Avoid hard shadows and glare.
  3. Start the camera with all pieces on their starting squares, then tap the four outer corners when asked.
  4. Just play. After each move, keep hands clear for about a second and the move is read automatically.
